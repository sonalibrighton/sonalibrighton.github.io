## Project: Trace Metal and VOC Analysis of Latex Paint

**Focus:** 

Conducted a multi-week analytical research project with two other group members to evaluate trace metals and volatile organic compounds (VOCs) in commercially available water-based latex paint. Prepared and analyzed samples using ICP-OES for elemental analysis and gas chromatography for VOC characterization, while developing calibration methods and evaluating analytical performance. The study also compared green-certified and non-green-certified paint products to investigate differences in potentially hazardous constituents.

**Key Contributions:**

- Developed and maintained the project Gantt chart to track experimental activities, workflow, and project progress.
- Performed extensive preparation of dried paint samples for elemental analysis.
- Cut and grinded substrate-supported paint samples prior to digestion.
- Supported acid digestion and dissolution of prepared samples for ICP-OES analysis.
- Operated the ICP-OES extensively and collected multiple sets of analytical data.

## Project: Pilot-Scale Process Control and Instrumentation Project

 **Focus:** 

This three-member capstone project involved recommissioning and modifying a recirculating pilot plant, calibrating flow and concentration instrumentation, developing an updated Process & Instrumentation Diagram (P&ID), assembling a PID temperature-control system, and implementing PLC-based on-off concentration control. We also evaluated closed-loop response, disturbance recovery, and process variability using control charts and statistical performance measures.

**Key Contributions:**

I personally completed much of the hands-on mechanical work during the pilot plant recommissioning and modification. This included measuring and cutting piping, soldering connections, fitting and installing equipment, assembling tubing and fittings, completing PVC solvent welding, and modifying sections of the process piping to accommodate new instrumentation and process equipment.

I also supported the physical installation and integration of pumps, valves, piping, and instrumentation within the recirculating system before calibration and control testing. This gave me direct hands-on experience with pilot-scale process equipment, piping assembly, equipment fitting, and plant modification work.

## Project: Qualification & Implementation of Automation for Core Imaging Process

**Focus:**

Qualification and implementation of an automated Laser Direct Imaging workflow for PCB core processing. The project focused on integrating imaging and material-handling equipment, validating process performance across production conditions, troubleshooting equipment and system issues, reducing manual operator intervention, and improving process consistency, equipment utilization, and manufacturing efficiency.

**Key Contributions:**

During my Process Engineering internship at TTM Technologies, I supported the qualification and implementation of an automated Laser Direct Imaging (LDI) workflow used for PCB core processing. My individual contributions included coordinating equipment integration and troubleshooting with internal technical teams and equipment vendors, supporting system setup and configuration, and carrying out production qualification testing across multiple process and material conditions.

I personally performed qualification work on both the imaging equipment and the automated material-handling system. This included verifying imaging performance, evaluating target recognition and process conditions, and confirming that the automation system could reliably handle the required range of production materials. I also supported live production trials and reviewed manufacturing performance data to evaluate the implementation. This included monitoring measures such as first-pass yield, throughput, equipment utilization, and defect trends to identify issues and assess process performance.

As part of the implementation, I developed and updated standard operating procedures (SOPs), qualification documentation, and operator guidance. I also helped train production operators on the automated workflow, equipment operation, and troubleshooting procedures.

The project required ongoing coordination with maintenance, engineering, equipment vendors, and production personnel to resolve integration issues and support the transition from a more manual imaging process to an automated workflow.

- Worked cross-functionally with engineering, maintenance, production, and vendor technical teams during implementation.
- Coordinated equipment integration and troubleshooting with internal teams and equipment vendors.
- Performed qualification testing on the LDI imaging system and automated material-handling equipment.
- Evaluated imaging performance, process conditions, target recognition, and material-handling capability.
- Supported live production trials and reviewed yield, throughput, equipment-utilization, and defect data.
- Assisted with troubleshooting system configuration and equipment-integration issues.
- Developed SOPs, qualification documentation, and operator procedures.
- Supported operator training for the new automated process.
